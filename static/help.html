Archigraph Help & Feature Guide

Archigraph reconstructs how organizations evolve over time—combining Wayback Machine historical captures, Gemini structured entity extraction, longitudinal diffing, VC/PE org diligence metrics, and executive recruiting signals in one interactive workspace.

Hover Tip: Hover or focus on any button, tab, pill, or ? badge in the workspace (and on this page) to reveal a contextual information bubble explaining what it does. Press Esc at any time to dismiss an open bubble.

1. Getting Started & Live Extraction

You can explore Archigraph immediately with pre-loaded longitudinal datasets or extract live organizational hierarchies from public company team pages.

Quick Company Demos

Click Google, Meta, SpaceX, Impulse Space, or Palantir in the top bar to load 4-era longitudinal benchmarks (2018 → 2024), or click Replit (extracted) to inspect a real Gemini extraction of an archived Wayback snapshot compared against Replit's current leadership page.

Live Wayback + Gemini Extraction

Paste any public leadership or team URL (for example, https://replit.com/about), pick 1–5 Snapshots, and click Extract Network. Archigraph queries the Wayback Machine CDX API (deduplicated by monthly content digest), cleans HTML boilerplate, and extracts structured profiles via gemini-3.8-flash.

Access Tokens & Rate Guardrails

Hosted deployments gate live Gemini calls behind ARCHIGRAPH_ACCESS_TOKEN. On your first live extraction, the browser prompts once for the token and stores it in sessionStorage for your current tab session. If Wayback rate-limits the newest capture, Archigraph automatically falls back to the live URL.

Data Provenance & Source Verification

Every extracted profile records its Line Confidence (Explicit, Inferred, or Root Node) and a quoted justification. Click any person and select Verify Snapshot Source ↗ to inspect the original Wayback capture or live webpage.

The interactive D3.js canvas supports zooming, panning, subtree folding, search-to-zoom, and two complementary network topologies.

3. Investor Diligence & Recruiting Intelligence

Whenever an organization is loaded, the right-hand Investor & Search Signals hub computes four institutional lenses for the active snapshot era:

VC / PE Org Scorecard

  • Retention Rate & VP+ Churn: Measures continuity of the prior snapshot's leadership cohort and isolates Director+/VP+/C-suite exits.
  • Bench Velocity Archetype: Classifies leadership growth as Internal Bench-Builder, External Heavy, or Balanced based on internal promotions vs. external senior hires.
  • Max Span & Unfilled VP+ Seats: Flags managers with overloaded direct-report spans and senior roles vacated without a replacement.

Executive Poachability Radar (0–100)

  • Layered Over in Re-Org: A new intermediate executive was inserted above an existing leader.
  • Sponsor / Manager Departed: The executive's prior reporting manager exited the company.
  • Stagnant Amid Peer Promotions: Title remained static across snapshots while peers advanced in seniority.
  • Scope / Span Compression: Direct report count contracted relative to earlier eras.

Hiring & Job-Board Signal Fusion

Paste raw job descriptions or fetch a live public Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby board URL. Archigraph parses explicit reporting clauses ("Reporting directly to our CTO…"), maps open seats onto the hiring manager, and extracts roadmap signals (e.g., CUDA, AI Agents, FedRAMP).

Backchannels & Contact Matching

Click My Network (or load the 9-contact demo VC network) to match your LinkedIn CSV or CRM list against both current leaders and departed alumni—surfacing off-list reference checks with exact historical overlap eras alongside current executives.

4. Warm Paths, Blast Radius, Portfolio & Exports

Warm Path Finder

Select any two people in Warm Path Finder (or click Warm Path from Top Exec on a profile card) to illuminate the shortest introduction chain—prioritizing formal reporting edges first, shared expertise second, and department peers third.

Key-Person Departure Simulator

Select any active leader and click Simulate Departure to model a what-if exit directly on the graph: orphaned direct reports, downstream flight-risk shock, single-point domain expertise loss, and ranked internal succession candidates.

Portfolio Screener & Spinout Tracker

Click Portfolio Screener in the header to sort all tracked companies by Retention %, VP+ Churn, Prime Targets, or Open Roles—and automatically trace cross-company alumni founder spinouts (such as spacex.com → impulsespace.com).

Watchlist Alerts & Institutional Exports

Open Alerts to toggle trigger rules and copy production-ready Slack Block Kit or CRM / ATS Event JSON webhooks. Use Export ▾ to download a VC & Search Brief (.md), Temporal JSON, Snapshot Roster CSV, or standalone Vector SVG.

5. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Why is the URL extraction box disabled on some deployments?

By default, hosted instances set ENABLE_LIVE_EXTRACTION=false so demos run at zero API cost. To enable live crawling on Render or Docker, set ENABLE_LIVE_EXTRACTION=true and configure GEMINI_API_KEY in your service environment variables.

Where do I find the access token when prompted during live extraction?

In your Render dashboard, open your Archigraph web service, click Environment in the left sidebar, and reveal the auto-generated ARCHIGRAPH_ACCESS_TOKEN value. Paste it once when prompted; Archigraph saves it in sessionStorage for the rest of your browser tab session.

What happens if the Wayback Machine doesn't have snapshots or rate-limits a URL?

Archigraph automatically retries transient Wayback 429 / 503 responses with exponential backoff, and falls back to scraping the live target URL (current) whenever archive captures are unavailable or rate-limited—without deducting failed HTTP downloads from your daily Gemini extraction budget.

What is the difference between solid and dashed lines on the org chart?

Solid lines represent explicit reporting relationships directly stated in source text or structured data. Dashed lines indicate reporting lines inferred from page layout/title hierarchy or, in Relationship Mesh mode, shared-expertise bridges between peers.

Why did the first page load take 30–60 seconds on Render?

Render's free tier spins containers down after periods of inactivity. Once awake, demo switching, pathfinding, diligence scoring, and departure simulations respond in milliseconds. Note that free-tier disk storage is ephemeral, so live-extracted caches reset when the container restarts—use Export ▾ → Temporal JSON to keep permanent offline copies.
